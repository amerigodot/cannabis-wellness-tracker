# Track Spec: Enhance Edge AI Reasoning

## Overview
This track focuses on improving the quality, accuracy, and factual grounding of the AI-generated clinical summaries. By refining the prompt engineering and the underlying feature engineering, we aim to provide clinicians with more reliable decision support.

## Goals
- Improve the factual grounding of AI summaries by integrating localized clinical protocols.
- Refine the clinical feature engineering to provide higher-signal data to the LLM.
- Implement a deterministic "grounding check" to catch and flag potential AI hallucinations.
- Ensure the AI responses strictly follow the clinical SOAP note format.

## Scope
- `src/hooks/useClinicalSummarizer.ts`: Refine the prompt template and engine configuration.
- `src/utils/clinicalAugmentation.ts`: Enhance the `computeClinicalFeatures` function for better signal extraction.
- `src/utils/clinicalGrounding.ts` (New): Implement deterministic verification of AI claims.
- `src/data/clinicalProtocols.ts`: Ensure protocols are structured for efficient RAG.

## Technical Details
- **Prompt Engineering:** Use In-Context Learning (ICL) to inject specific clinical guidelines (e.g., ACOEM, LRCUG) into the prompt.
- **Low Temperature:** Maintain `temperature: 0.1` for high determinism.
- **Grounding Check:** Compare the LLM's risk flag mentions against the deterministic risk flags generated by `computeClinicalFeatures`.

## Success Metrics
- 100% of summaries must include a clinical disclaimer.
- >95% alignment between deterministic risk flags and LLM-reported risks.
- Average summary length remains under 150 words.
