# Initial Concept
A private, intelligent journal to track medical, recreational, and wellness cannabis use. Monitor effects, discover patterns, and leverage AI-powered insights to find what works best for you.

# Vision
The Cannabis Wellness Tracker aims to bridge the gap between patient self-care and clinical oversight in the medical cannabis space. By utilizing cutting-edge Edge AI technology, the platform provides deep clinical insights while maintaining the highest standards of patient privacy.

# Core Goals
- **Privacy-First Tracking:** Ensure sensitive health and substance-use data remains on the user's device through Local-Only Inference.
- **Clinical Decision Support:** Provide AI-driven analysis of usage patterns to improve therapeutic outcomes and shared decision-making.
- **Secure Collaboration:** Facilitate a safe channel for patients to share high-signal trends with their healthcare providers via granular consent scopes.
- **Harm Reduction:** Proactively identify risky usage patterns (like Dose Drift) and provide localized AI guidance.

# Target Audience
- **Medical Cannabis Patients:** Individuals seeking to optimize their treatment plans and understand strain/dosage efficacy.
- **Clinicians & Healthcare Providers:** Doctors who need reliable, patient-reported data to manage cannabis-based therapies safely.

# Key Features
- **Intelligent Journaling:** Localized logging of dosages, strains, and symptomatic effects.
- **Edge AI Summarization:** In-browser clinical reasoning using WebLLM and Gemma-2B to generate SOAP notes and trend analysis.
- **Clinician Dashboard:** A professional interface for monitoring patient adherence and risk flags without centralizing raw data.
- **Privacy-Preserving Linking:** 6-digit one-time codes for secure, E2EE patient-clinician data sharing.
- **Localized RAG:** AI advice grounded in locally stored clinical literature for high factual accuracy.
